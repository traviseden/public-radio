# Census inputs for U.S. radio-contour reach — snapshot, November 21, 2026

## Which source answers which reach question?

### Takeaway
Use **2020 DHC + 2020 blocks** for the finest published residential population, age-band and housing footprint. Use **2020–2024 ACS 5-year Detailed Tables + 2024 TIGER/Line block groups or tracts** for socioeconomic context. Report these as separate vintages and statistical measures. Neither measures listeners, listening time, devices, signal reception indoors, or the number of people physically present at broadcast time. [DHC contents](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2023/dec/2020-census-dhc.html) · [ACS table inventory](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/technical-documentation/table-shells/2024.html)

### Cited findings
- **Time:** DHC describes the April 1, 2020 enumeration; released May 25, 2023. ACS pooled and reweighted 60 months of sample data, January 2020–December 2024; released January 29, **2026**. It is a period estimate, neither a December 2024 headcount nor the sum or simple mean of five published annual estimates. As of the snapshot, Census lists the **2025 one-year** release October 29, 2026 but the **2025 five-year** release date as TBD. The one-year product cannot replace block-group coverage. [Decennial products](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/data-products.2020.html) · [2024 ACS release](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/news/data-releases/2024/release.html) · [multiyear accuracy and weighting](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/accuracy/MultiyearACSAccuracyofData2024.pdf) · [2025 schedule](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/news/data-releases/2025/release-schedule.html)
- **Statistical uncertainty:** ACS has sampling and nonsampling error. Its published MOE reflects sampling variability at 90% confidence (`SE = MOE / 1.645`); small subgroup estimates can have MOEs comparable to the estimate. Pandemic-related reduced interviews generally increased MOEs in five-year periods including 2020; revised weighting addresses nonresponse bias. ACS also applies confidentiality edits, including swapping and suppression. DHC is an enumeration, not an ACS sample: no analogous sampling MOE. Counts remain subject to coverage, response, processing/imputation and disclosure-avoidance error. Disclosure avoidance uses differential privacy; tiny block counts and cross-tabs can be implausible. Census recommends aggregating **DHC and P.L.** small populations/geographies. [ACS accuracy](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/accuracy/MultiyearACSAccuracyofData2024.pdf) · [DHC guidance and confidentiality](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2023/dec/2020-census-dhc.html) · [2020 coverage measurement](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/about/coverage-measurement.html)
- **ACS-derived totals:** For 134 selected detailed tables, 80 variance replicates allow aggregates, ratios and percentages to account for covariance; check table **and** geography availability. Otherwise apply Census approximation formulas and label the result approximate. For a sum of distinct areas with fixed allocation fractions `wᵢ`, the independence approximation is `sqrt(sum((wᵢ × MOEᵢ)²))`; apply the same weights to each replicate when available. Neither this number nor a published Census MOE describes uncertainty in where people live *inside* a polygon or error in the contour. [VRE documentation](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/replicate_estimates/2024/documentation/5-year/2020-2024_Variance_Replicate_Table_Documentation.pdf) · [ACS accuracy/approximations](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/accuracy/MultiyearACSAccuracyofData2024.pdf)
- **Scope:** Standard state files cover 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico (Puerto Rico Community Survey for ACS); define whether PR is in the station footprint and handle PR-specific tables. Island Areas are separate decennial products; TIGER coverage alone does not establish a matching ACS or DHC row. [ACS developer coverage](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/acs-5year.2024.html) · [decennial products](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/data-products.2020.html)

### Inferences
- Label outputs “estimated residents within modeled contour,” with population reference period, contour date and allocation rule visible. Group quarters residents are people but not households. A seasonal/vacant unit is not an occupied household. Commuting data describe workers, not cars tuned to radio. [DHC topic definitions](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2023/dec/2020-census-dhc.html) · [ACS commuting metadata](https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5/groups/B08301.html)

### Gaps
- Neither public source observes within-block addresses of residents or actual listening. No hit-rate or error bound for the station's coverage model follows from these tables.

## What is actually available at each geographic level?

### Takeaway
Table **and product** determine the finest geography. DHC `P`/`H` reach block; `PCT`/`HCT` stop at tract; `PCO` stops at county. ACS never publishes these estimates for blocks. Its `B`/`C` prefix does **not** determine whether block groups are published. [DHC technical documentation, chapters 1–3](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/technical-documentation/complete-tech-docs/demographic-and-housing-characteristics-file-and-demographic-profile/2020census-demographic-and-housing-characteristics-file-and-demographic-profile-techdoc.pdf) · [2024 ACS Table List, column G](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/table_shells/table_lists/2024_DataProductList.xlsx)

### Inferences
- If a contour needs education × age × income **jointly**, separate marginal tables cannot establish how many individuals have all three traits. Choose an actual published cross-tab, use a coarser geography, or omit the combination. ACS public-use microdata identify PUMAs rather than individual blocks/tracts. [ACS release/product levels](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/news/data-releases/2024/release-schedule.html)

### Gaps
- Table/geography eligibility above is metadata verification, not a completeness audit of every local cell. A suppressed, unavailable or undefined value is distinct from a published zero.

## How should a reproducible contour extraction work?

### Takeaway
Pin the statistical release, geography vintage, table, variable, universe and key format **before** overlay. Census provides both local API queries and bulk files; no nationwide statistical or geometry file was downloaded for these notes.

### Cited findings
1. **ACS path:** query `https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5` (not `acs1`, `profile`, `subject` or PUMS). A county-scale query string is `?get=NAME,B01003_001E,B01003_001M&for=block%20group:*&in=state:06%20county:073&key=YOUR_KEY`; for tract-only tables use `for=tract:*` with state and county. All data calls now require a personal Census API key; examples, geography and group metadata are public. Alternatively the [table-based bulk directory](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2024/table-based-SF/data/5YRData/) has one pipe-delimited `.dat` per table, e.g. `acsdt5y2024-b01003.dat` (all available geographies). Select only the needed tables; filter standard full geographies by `15000US` / `14000US` and state; GEO_ID suffix is the 12-/11-character key. If names needed, [Geos20245YR.txt and shells](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2024/table-based-SF/documentation/). Use actual bulk header names, not assumed API field names. [Official API examples](https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5/examples.html) · [summary file format](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/summary-file.2024.html)
2. **ACS geometry:** select intersecting states' full-resolution [TIGER2024/BG](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2024/BG/) `tl_2024_SS_bg.zip` or [TIGER2024/TRACT](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2024/TRACT/) `tl_2024_SS_tract.zip`. Join to `GEOID` = state(2)+county(3)+tract(6)[+BG(1)]; keep strings/leading zeroes. The end year determines ACS geographic vintage; legal boundaries as of Jan 1, 2024, TIGER release Sept 25, 2024. Statistical areas derive principally from Census 2020 delineation but boundary corrections and legal changes matter. Connecticut's nine planning-region county equivalents replacing eight counties are a concrete reason not to truncate a 2020 block ID and join it blindly to a 2024 BG. [TIGER release](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2024/geo/tiger-line-file.html) · [schema, appendix G](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2024/TGRSHP2024_TechDoc_F-S.pdf) · [ACS geography vintages](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/geography-acs/geography-boundaries-by-year.html) · [Connecticut change](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/technical-documentation/user-notes/2023-01.html)
3. **DHC path:** query `https://api.census.gov/data/2020/dec/dhc?get=NAME,P1_001N,H1_001N,H3_002N&for=block:*&in=state:06%20county:037%20tract:*&key=YOUR_KEY`. Use the supported state/county/tract hierarchy, narrow to a single intersecting tract if needed. For offline use get just affected states from the [DHC bulk directory](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/data/demographic-and-housing-characteristics-file/) (e.g. [DC directory: dc2020.dhc.zip](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/data/demographic-and-housing-characteristics-file/District_of_Columbia/)). The geographic header + 44 pipe-delimited segments are state file sets. Use the segment/table matrix; link the chosen segment to its own state geo header via `LOGRECNO` within state and iteration; select DHC summary level **`100`** blocks (`150` BG, `140` tract), full geographic component. Do not join on segment line number or sum mixed summary levels. [API examples](https://api.census.gov/data/2020/dec/dhc/examples.html) · [file layout and geography](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/technical-documentation/complete-tech-docs/demographic-and-housing-characteristics-file-and-demographic-profile/2020census-demographic-and-housing-characteristics-file-and-demographic-profile-techdoc.pdf)
4. **DHC geometry:** use official [2020 tabulation blocks](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020PL/LAYER/TABBLOCK/2020/) `tl_2020_SS_tabblock20.zip` and `GEOID20` = state(2)+county(3)+tract(6)+block(4), **15 characters**. For the tract or BG use [2020 tracts](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020PL/LAYER/TRACT/2020/) / [2020 BG](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020PL/LAYER/BG/2020/). The DC DBF in the linked `TIGER2020PL` directory has geometry IDs but **no counts**. Shortcut when only total people and units needed: the distinct [TIGER2020/TABBLOCK20](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020/TABBLOCK20/) directory (same filename, different contents) supplies **2020 tabulation blocks with population and housing counts**; its files carry `POP20`, `HOUSING20`; these do not mean occupied units or age × sex. The 2024 TIGER release also contains a `tabblock20` layer with these 2020 attributes, distinct from **current suffixed blocks** (`tabblocksufx`), which can split old blocks and have no population/housing counts. Inspect actual layer attributes before relying on the shortcut. [2020 counts-file schema](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/technical-documentation/complete-technical-documentation/tiger-geo-line/2020.html) · [2024 TIGER §4.2–4.3](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2024/TGRSHP2024_TechDoc.pdf) · [2024 appendix G field layouts](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2024/TGRSHP2024_TechDoc_F-S.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Allocation:** dissolve overlapping contours first for “unique residents potentially covered”; keep them separate for per-station estimates. Full geographic units contribute their entire additive count. For partial units compare internal-point assignment with a land-area fraction in an appropriate equal-area projection; geometric intersection alone does not locate people. Avoid treating polygon coverage over rivers/lakes as residential land. A rural block can be very large; an internal point need not be a population center. Report edge sensitivity, including the fully-contained vs all-intersected sums for nonnegative counts, separately from sampling MOE. [TIGER block definitions, land/water and internal point fields](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2024/TGRSHP2024_TechDoc.pdf)
- **Optional hybrid:** spatially reconcile 2020 inhabited blocks with 2024 ACS BG/tract polygons. Compute the fraction of each ACS source area's 2020 residents (people variables) or occupied units (household variables) inside the contour; allocate its additive ACS estimate by that fraction. Check splits, zero denominators, boundary drift and new development. This assumes within-area subgroup distribution follows the proxy; label as modeled, never “block-level ACS.” Compute rates from separately allocated numerator/eligible denominator; do not sum percentages, average medians, or silently substitute housing units for people. [Census area-change warning](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2024/TGRSHP2024_TechDoc.pdf) · [ACS table universes](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/technical-documentation/table-shells/2024.html)
- **Validation/checkpoint:** record product/path, reference dates, retrieval date, input hashes, geographic subset, GEOID and summary-level rules, table shell/restriction version, errors/annotations, allocation, CRS and contour version. Check one-to-one keys, unmatched IDs both directions, complete state coverage, aggregate to known published same-vintage totals for a fully covered test area, and investigate rather than silently discarding discrepancies. Store separate results for 2020 counts and ACS estimates/MOEs.
