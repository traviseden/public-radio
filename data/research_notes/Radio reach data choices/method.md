# Estimating demographic reach from FCC FM contours

## 1. Which footprint and population are we estimating?

### Takeaway

Report **estimated residents or households inside an eligible modeled FM service footprint**, as of a named FCC snapshot and demographic period. Resolve the operating facility and its authorizations before intersecting anything. This measures potential geographic reach, not listeners, actual reception at a house, or the number of people served by a grantee.

### Cited findings

- The project starts with dated LMS downloads but a mutable `FM_service_contour_current.zip`; AM is not in this feed. FCC contours are calculated points; service contour field strengths vary by station class/channel (including 54, 57 and 60 dBu). A service polygon is not a universal common-strength threshold. [Project download instructions](../../README.md), [FCC contour definitions](https://www.fcc.gov/media/radio/fm-service-contour-data-points), [FCC map interpretation](https://www.fcc.gov/media/radio/general-info-fm-tv-maps-data).
- The importer de-duplicates identical rows, retains original point strings and emits longitude–latitude GeoJSON; it closes the ring but does not establish polygon validity or authorization eligibility. Its unique key is `(lms_application_id,dts_site_number)`, not `facility_id`. The existing Datasette `contour_candidates` query joins via active `application_facility` and enriches with `license_filing_version`. [Importer](../../scripts/create_db.py), [exact query](../../metadata.yaml).
- Read-only snapshot check (2026-11-21): 27,134 contours with non-null geometry, including 2,818 `FS` auxiliary records. Across **all** services in the contour table, 22,979 facility IDs linked via active application-facility rows; 3,278 had multiple candidate rows (maximum seven). Contour-linked filings included 24,817 `L/GRA`, 1,810 `C/GRA` and 109 `C/PEN`. WBHM (facility `4240`) has a granted license contour whose application ID differs from `facility.latest_filing_version_id`; requiring equality would drop it. These are snapshot diagnostics, not counts of public stations or usable facilities. [Local SQLite source](../../public_radio_facility_data.sqlite), [column/join definitions](../../metadata.yaml). Reproduce the multiplicity diagnostic with:

  ```sql
  WITH candidate AS (
    SELECT DISTINCT af.afac_facility_id id, c.rowid rid
    FROM fm_service_contour c JOIN application_facility af
      ON af.afac_application_id=c.lms_application_id WHERE af.active_ind='Y'
  ), n AS (SELECT id,count(*) k FROM candidate GROUP BY id)
  SELECT count(*),sum(k>1),max(k) FROM n;
  ```
- Station index rows mix radio and TV; even the WBHM `FCCID` is a filing number, not numeric facility ID. `station_ownership` is a respondent/FRN join; station YAML separately records ultimate owner and can be an unfilled stub. [Index](../../station_index.csv), [ownership view](../../scripts/create_db.py), [WBHM stub](../../stations/WBHM.yaml).

### Inferences / recommended policy

- Resolve the in-scope roster to dated facility IDs (callsign, service and filing corroboration); persist ambiguous/unmatched separately. Distinguish station/program network, licensee and controlling owner. Use evidence for translator rebroadcast relationships, not geographic proximity or common ownership. Owner-level reach and program-network reach answer different questions.
- Implement a versioned eligibility ledger: candidate key, facility, facility status, service, authority type, purpose/status/effective dates, evidence URL, selected/excluded/unresolved reason and snapshot. Start with licensed in-scope FM/LPFM primaries, checking license in force, supersession, silent status where known and site validity against [FCC LMS](https://enterpriseefiling.fcc.gov/dataentry/public/tv/lmsDatabase.html) and [FCC FM Query](https://www.fcc.gov/media/radio/fm-query). `GRA` alone includes permits. Do not assume newest filing or largest area is operating. Exclude proposals, unbuilt permits, superseded facilities and auxiliary backups from default; document treatment of verified temporary operations/STA. Count translator/booster coverage only when eligible and linked under the declared definition. Expose alternate scenarios where unresolved; no contour ≠ zero reach.
- Union **concurrently eligible** polygons for a facility before counting; union across included facilities for owner/network unique reach, then compute fresh weights. Alternative authorizations are scenarios, not simultaneous sites. Summing station totals counts overlapping residents multiple times; capping summed area fractions at one is also wrong where two stations cover the same half of an area.
- Intersect actual footprint bounds with a declared geography universe, not only license-state, transmitter county, grantee address or CONUS. Use all intersected states/county equivalents, DC, AK, HI, and PR where the selected table/universe is comparable. Other island areas require a separate source/period decision; flag foreign and unsupported land beyond data extent, and exclude water from land diagnostics. Never renormalize the measured U.S. total to the full contour. Keep the full map outline plus measured extent/partial-coverage flag; do not infer residents abroad from a land-area ratio. [ACS geographic coverage](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/acs-5year.html), [Island Areas Censuses](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/decade/2020/planning-management/release/about-2020-island-area-censuses.html).

### Gaps

No adjudicated roster of operating sites, rebroadcast links or effective-dated owners was established here. Retrieval of the FCC contour documentation itself returned 403; service-class threshold examples were visible in FCC-indexed text. Verify the datum of the specific archived file (not simply a GeoJSON default), including any legacy records, before overlay.

## 2. How should partial coverage and demographic uncertainty be handled?

### Takeaway

Use additive ACS 5-year detailed-table counts at the smallest supported geography; compare three ways to allocate boundary units. Prefer block population support for people, occupied housing support for households where available, and an explicit fallback flag. More detailed support improves the location proxy, not the resolution of the survey.

### Cited findings

- ACS does not provide block-level estimates; block groups are the lowest published level and availability depends on product/table. Five-year estimates describe a period. Match boundary geography to the selected release, not to the download year. [ACS geography handbook](https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/publications/2020/acs/acs_geography_handbook_2020_ch01.pdf), [geography by vintage](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/geography-acs/geography-boundaries-by-year.html).
- Updated 2020 TIGER/Line tabulation blocks carry population/housing counts and internal points. Population includes disclosure-avoidance noise. `HOUSING20` is housing **units**, not occupied households; 2020 PL table H1 distinguishes occupied/vacant. [TIGER release and revision](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2020/geo/tiger-line-file.html), [record layout](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/technical-documentation/complete-technical-documentation/tiger-geo-line/2020.html), [H1 variables](https://api.census.gov/data/2020/dec/pl/groups/H1.html).
- Census gives approximate derived-count, proportion and ratio MOE procedures, and 80 variance replicates for selected five-year tables/geographies to incorporate covariance. Census medians may be suppressed. Aggregation scale and zoning can change ecological inference (MAUP). [Derived estimates handbook, ch. 8](https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/publications/2020/acs/acs_general_handbook_2020_ch08.pdf), [variance replicate coverage and documentation](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/variance-tables.html), [median user note](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/technical-documentation/user-notes/2016-01.html), [MAUP study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12714233/).

### Inferences / comparison

For source geography `g`, footprint `F`, count `Xg`, estimate `sum(wg × Xg)` with a single weight per geography/footprint/universe. Full inclusion = 1, disjoint = 0. For boundary units:

| Method | Weight | What can go wrong / use |
|---|---|---|
| Representative point | 1 if F covers the tract/BG internal point, else 0. A geometric centroid can be outside its polygon. | Cheapest reproducible baseline; can miss a whole settlement or include a large rural area on a small boundary change. |
| Intersection / areal | Any positive-area overlap counts a unit as *touched*, not its entire population. For estimation, divide intersected land area by total land area using the same land geometry and appropriate equal-area CRS or geodesic areas. | Fractional allocation assumes uniform density on land. Coastal water, parks and thin slivers matter. A scalar `ALAND` denominator does not remove water from the intersected numerator. Use full-polygon area only if explicitly labeled. |
| Inhabited block support | `wg = Σb(qb × sb) / Σb qb` within g. `qb` = 2020 block residents for person counts; occupied units for household counts; all units for housing-unit counts. `sb` = 0/1 by internal point, or covered land fraction for split blocks. | Places more weight where the census counted people. Blocks are not address points: a large rural block, prison, campus, recent subdivision or seasonal housing can dominate. Binary “inhabited” alone weights a five-resident block like a 500-resident block. Compare internal-point and split-block variants. |

- For a mixed-vintage block/BG join, verify actual nesting, identifiers, totals and boundaries. Use official [relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/relationship-files.html) when applicable or explicit overlay/reassignment; never assume truncating an older GEOID works nationally (e.g., changed county equivalents). Record shares for split supports without duplicating mass. Zero support with nonzero ACS count is unresolved: carry a flagged land-area fallback or omit with a completeness flag, not a fabricated zero. Use an appropriate regional projection for AK/HI/PR and split antimeridian-crossing shapes before indexing; check axis order, holes, validity, repairs and area change.
- Preserve universe (persons, households, persons of a given age, poverty universe, etc.) and counts. For a rate, compute `100 × ΣwN / ΣwD` for matching numerator/denominator, never average geography percentages. Where only total-person support exists for a subgroup, explicitly assume its within-unit distribution follows people, not that group membership is known. Use published joint cross-tabs if available. Marginal race and income rates do not determine low-income residents by race: for two groups of sizes A,B in the same universe T the possible overlap spans `max(0,A+B−T)` to `min(A,B)`. Do not synthesize an independence-based joint count or infer individual listeners from neighborhoods. For unavailable tables use coarser published geography as a labeled separate estimate, not a silent substitution.
- Store source estimate, 90% MOE, annotation, unit/universe, geography and period. Decode release-specific sentinel/annotation values; missing/suppressed is not zero. For a weighted count with fixed weights, conditional approximate `MOE90 ≈ sqrt(Σ(wg × Mg)^2)` neglecting covariance. Collapse repeated contributions to the same source estimate **before** squaring. Use matched replicate aggregates for weighted rates when available; `SE = sqrt((4/80) Σ(θr−θ)^2)`, `MOE90=1.645 SE`, subject to Census zero-estimate/special-case guidance. Otherwise follow handbook subset-proportion versus general-ratio formulas (use ratio fallback for a negative radicand); report undefined zero denominators. Do not average MOEs. These intervals measure survey sampling conditional on weights, not signal uncertainty, spatial allocation, disclosure avoidance or time mismatch. Show allocation-method spread separately; it is not a confidence interval.
- Do not combine tract median incomes (even household-weighted) into a station median: aggregate [B19001 income-bin counts](https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5/groups/B19001.html) and report the median bracket or labeled interpolated approximation with dollar year and assumptions; open-ended median bins have no identified finite midpoint. Prefer distributions/threshold shares; suppress weak estimates under a documented rule. Retain full precision for calculations and round only for display.

### Gaps

The location of people within partial blocks and the location of specific demographic subgroups within ACS units are not observed here. Validate high-growth and group-quarters cases separately. A perfectly reproduced 2020 block benchmark does not validate a later ACS subgroup estimate or indoor reception.

## 3. What should be built and benchmarked after the choice?

### Takeaway

Precompute versioned footprint-to-geography weights and aggregates offline; use SQLite/Datasette for indexed lookup. Decide whether the extra block work earns its cost on a small, stratified pilot before a national ingest.

### Cited findings

- `cd data && make db` runs `uv run scripts/create_db.py`; the importer scans `tables/*.dat` and replaces same-name tables. `make launch-datasette` serves an immutable SQLite file with canned queries and a 20-second SQL limit. Current dependencies include pandas, PyYAML and Datasette plugins but no explicit spatial overlay engine. Generated SQLite/ZIP/DAT and `tables/*.txt` are ignored. [Makefile](../../Makefile), [importer](../../scripts/create_db.py), [dependency manifest](../../pyproject.toml), [ignore rules](../../.gitignore), [query metadata](../../metadata.yaml).
- Census provides both [ACS API](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/data-via-api.html) and [bulk Summary File](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/summary-file.html) access; select tables/geographies using release metadata. Neither Census tables nor live geography queries resolve which FCC polygons count.

### Inferences / proposed implementation and decision gate

1. **Declare and freeze inputs.** Commit a small YAML manifest (release, URLs, expected hashes, filenames, extraction rules, table/variable IDs, universe, GEOID width, geography vintage/scope, FCC policy). Keep raw archives outside `tables/*.dat`, e.g., ignored `data/downloads/reach/<release>/`. Record actual hashes, UTC retrieval times, source modification dates, row/byte counts and schema checks, including the mutable FCC file and actual LMS vintage. Pin Python/spatial packages via `uv.lock` and record projection/geometry-library versions. Separate an explicitly networked fetch from builds that run from archived bytes offline.
2. **Use the existing workflow without entangling it.** Proposed opt-in `make reach-fetch`, `reach-build`, `reach-check` under `data/`, invoking `uv run scripts/...` with explicit manifest/scope. Snapshot FCC SQLite as read-only input; write temporary `radio_reach.sqlite`, validate then atomically replace. Keep human station YAML for ownership evidence. Add the sidecar as a second immutable Datasette DB with its own metadata and indexed summary queries; include station labels in sidecar so ordinary queries need no cross-database joins.
3. **Store reusable layers.** Example keys: `release(run_id,source_hashes,period,geo_vintage,policy,method_version)`; `footprint_member(run_id,target_id,facility_id,application_id,site,decision,reason)`; `footprint(run_id,target_id,kind,geometry_hash,extent_status)`; `acs_cell(release,geolevel,geoid,variable,estimate,moe,annotation)`; `weight(run_id,target_id,geoid,method,support_kind,numerator,denominator,w,flag)`; `reach(run_id,target_id,metric,estimate,moe90,method,completeness)`. Use composite unique keys including method/support/release; text GEOIDs with leading zeros. Index both target-first and GEOID-first joins. Store audited mapping/evidence and input/output hashes separately from display geometry. Index bounding boxes or use a spatial tree offline, then exact predicates. Reuse weights for new variables of the same universe; invalidate on contour, ownership grouping, boundaries, support vintage or eligibility change.
4. **Online versus offline.** For a tiny exploratory pilot, cached, scoped API calls are convenient; log endpoint, predicates, variables, retrieval and checksums without secrets. Repeated interactive API/TIGERweb requests or browser overlays add latency, network limits, mixed versions and irreproducible results. For many stations prefer checked bulk releases and state partitions intersecting eligible footprints; preserve raw inputs, serve materialized results. Arbitrary user-selected networks require a fresh union or de-duplicated fine-support membership with defined boundary treatment; precompute common groups instead of adding dashboard rows.
5. **Pilot after approval.** Freeze a small case list: local WBHM `4240` for the single-contour join; a verified multi-candidate/auxiliary case; two overlapping translators/primaries under one owner; a dense metro alongside a small LPFM; a large rural/mountainous footprint; a coast and interstate border; AK/HI/PR and unavailable territory as scope allows. Start with total residents and households, one subgroup and one denominator-derived rate. Hold footprint/period constant; run BG point, BG land-area and block support, then repeat at tract scale for MAUP. Use **same-vintage** decennial block counts aggregated to BG as coarse inputs, and sums over known whole-block test zones as a conservation/reference test. For real contour cuts, report wholly-inside block population as a lower and all positive-area-intersecting block population as an upper allocation bound (conditional on those counts and geometry). Compare split-block and point estimates inside that interval. Do not call a block-point result ground truth.
6. **Score and decide.** For each case report counts, absolute and relative differences (absolute only for tiny/zero baselines), rate percentage-point differences, boundary population share, support fallback/missing share, eligibility exclusions, sampling MOE, runtime/RAM/disk and dashboard query latency. Visually inspect outliers against settlements/coastlines. Require weights in `[0,1]`, no duplicate source keys, full-unit sums reproduced within rounding, unions between largest member and sum of members for nonnegative counts, correct null/unsupported flags, no double counting, identical keyed results on offline rebuild. Agree tolerances **before** results (illustrative review triggers: >5% in total or >2 percentage points in a share, not accuracy guarantees). If material differences concentrate in populated boundary cells, use block-supported weights nationally, refining only split blocks; if land-area agrees within pre-agreed tolerances across strata and block cost is prohibitive, publish area-weighted results with method flag and exception review. Keep point estimates as diagnostics. Do not publish unresolved eligibility or incomplete geographic coverage as complete station totals.

### Gaps / handoff

Only FCC/local metadata probes and documentation research were run; no demographic files downloaded, pilot executed, or production files changed. Next decision: agree on primary-only versus verified rebroadcast footprint, territorial scope, pilot metric universes and acceptance tolerances. Runtime, storage, method spread and the best national trade-off remain unmeasured.
