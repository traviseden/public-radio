# Measure radio footprints before profiling residents

**Use 2020 Census blocks to answer “how many residents lived inside this modeled FM footprint?” and 2020–2024 ACS Detailed Tables to answer “what do we know about the population in and around it?”** For this site, the best end product is a hybrid: publish the two measures side by side, then use block-supported allocation for selected ACS estimates only if a pilot justifies it. The best standalone source for owner-level unique residential reach is the 2020 Census Demographic and Housing Characteristics File (DHC) with matching blocks; the best standalone source for a socioeconomic profile is the ACS with matching 2024 TIGER/Line block groups or tracts. DHC is finer **in space** for its block tables; ACS is richer **in subject matter** and describes a later period. Neither measures listening, and neither puts a particular resident at an address inside a cut polygon. Resolve the eligible transmitters, dissolve overlapping coverage, and compute resident estimates offline; the static Astro site needs only dated results and display outlines.

## Choose the source by the claim it must support

| Rank for this project | What to publish | What the choice buys | What it costs or leaves unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hybrid** | A 2020 resident/household footprint and a separately labeled 2020–24 profile. Start with independent overlays; optionally locate partial ACS areas using 2020 person or housing support. | Finest public residential baseline plus education, income, poverty, language and other context. Each measure keeps its own date and denominator. | Two releases, boundary reconciliation if borrowing support, and an assumption about within-area distribution. Blocks locate neither later construction nor individual subgroup members. Never label the result “block-level ACS.” |
| **2. DHC alone** | April 1, 2020 residents, age bands, race/origin, households, group quarters, occupied/vacant units and tenure where published. | Best first release for a map comparing owner footprints, especially small LPFM or populated edges. Block totals are additive; for just population and total units a counts-bearing block file suffices. | Dated baseline; bigger overlays; census coverage and privacy error. No income, employment, education, commuting, language at home, housing prices or internet subscriptions. |
| **3. ACS alone** | 2020–24 estimates and sampling margins for eligible block-group or tract variables. **Rank this first among standalone choices if socioeconomic context is the primary requirement.** | One consistent survey period and boundary vintage, broader topics and a rolling update path. Fewer polygons than blocks. | Coarse cuts can move an entire neighborhood in or out. Small-domain sampling margins can overwhelm differences between owners. A newer vintage is not necessarily a more accurate footprint. |

DHC describes usual residence on **April 1, 2020**, although it was released in May 2023. ACS pools and reweights interviews from **January 2020 through December 2024**; it is neither a December 2024 headcount nor the sum or simple average of five annual estimates. The 2024 five-year tables were released January 29, 2026. At this report's November 21, 2026 snapshot, the 2025 one-year release does not replace five-year small-area coverage; check the five-year release schedule before refreshing. Comparing a DHC number with an ACS number is not a clean estimate of growth; adjacent five-year releases also share four years of interviews. Pair every statistic with its own date, source and universe, rather than using a 2020 numerator with an ACS denominator. ([DHC products](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2023/dec/2020-census-dhc.html); [ACS release](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/news/data-releases/2024/release.html); [2025 schedule](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/news/data-releases/2025/release-schedule.html); [multiyear methods](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/accuracy/MultiyearACSAccuracyofData2024.pdf))

**“Detailed” does not determine the smallest geography.** Blocks nest into block groups (BGs) and tracts within the same tabulation vintage. DHC `P` and `H` tables go to blocks; `PCT` and `HCT` stop at tracts, `PCO` at counties. ACS publishes no block estimates. A `B` detailed table and a `C` collapsed table can have different geographic restrictions even on the same topic. Use column G (“5-Year Geography Restrictions”) and the published summary-level sheet in the [2024 ACS table list](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/table_shells/table_lists/2024_DataProductList.xlsx), the [ACS shells and universes](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2024/table-based-SF/documentation/ACS20245YR_Table_Shells.txt), and the [DHC table guide](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/program-management/data-table-guide-dhc-dp.xlsx). Geographic eligibility is not a guarantee of a usable cell.

| Race and Hispanic origin, all residents | `P4` origin, `P8` race, `P9` origin × race. | `B03002_012E` Hispanic, any race; `_003E` non-Hispanic White alone. Use mutually exclusive cells; ethnicity overlaps race. |

Group-quarters residents are people, not households; a seasonal unit is not an occupied household. |

| Work, travel and connectivity | No corresponding measures. | `B08301` commute mode among workers 16+; `B23025_005E / _003E` unemployed / civilian labor force. `B28002_004E` households with broadband. These do not measure vehicles listening, daytime presence or radios. |

Store **dataset + table + variable**, not just the short name. The separate block-level P.L. 94-171 file offers race, origin, voting-age, major group-quarters and occupancy counts in six tables. P.L. `P1` is race (DHC `P8`); P.L. `H1` is occupancy (DHC `H3`), not DHC total units. Detailed DHC-A adds counts and sex/age for roughly 1,500 detailed groups; Detailed DHC-B adds household type and tenure by householder group. Their finest possible level is tract, subject to group thresholds, and Census recommends minimizing custom additions/subtractions as noise accumulates. The Demographic Profile also stops at tract. Public-use ACS microdata locate respondents only to larger PUMAs, not blocks. Separate marginal tables cannot identify, for example, low-income residents of a particular race: choose a published joint cross-tab at its supported geography or omit the claim. ([P.L. groups](https://api.census.gov/data/2020/dec/pl/groups.html); [DHC comparison](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2023/dec/2020-census-dhc.html); [Detailed A](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2023/dec/2020-census-detailed-dhc-a.html); [Detailed B](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2024/dec/2020-census-detailed-dhc-b.html); [field-level source notes](../research_notes/Radio%20reach%20data%20choices/census.md))

## Match boundaries to the release before joining counts

**A bounded local acquisition already works.** From the repository root, run the following target; leave `CENSUS_API_KEY` unset for the keyless path, or export your own key to request the optional observation CSVs. It writes archives, dictionaries and a hash manifest under `data/downloads/census-sample/`, not into the FCC database. The keyless run fetched the 2024 DC BGs, 2020 DC blocks and `B01003`/`P1` dictionaries. DBF inspection found **571 BGs, 6,012 blocks, 4,500 blocks with population, 689,545 people and 350,364 housing units**. The last two totals came from block attributes, not the dictionaries or a successful keyed query. DC has roughly ten times as many blocks as BGs; that illustrates the potential gain at a boundary, not a tenfold accuracy gain. DC alone also cannot cover a DC station whose signal extends into Maryland or Virginia. No keyed data call, national download, reach overlay or national performance benchmark was run. ## Count a resident once per owner, with boundary error visible

**Select authorizations before selecting residents.** The mutable FCC “current” feed contains calculated FM service boundaries, not AM contours or a verified public-radio roster. Field-strength thresholds vary by class/channel; none guarantees reception at a house, indoors, behind terrain or in a moving car. The importer closes longitude–latitude rings but does not validate topology or decide which filing is operating. Its contour key is `(lms_application_id, dts_site_number)`; join `application_facility.afac_application_id` to the **LMS** ID, use `afac_facility_id` for the facility, retain site as text and archive the FCC/LMS snapshot. The local inventory found **27,134** geometries, **2,818** `FS` auxiliary rows and **3,278 of 22,979** linked facilities with multiple candidates. At WBHM (`4240`), requiring equality to the facility's latest filing ID would discard a granted license contour. Those figures are diagnostics, not station counts. ([FCC contour definition](https://www.fcc.gov/media/radio/fm-service-contour-data-points); [FCC map interpretation](https://www.fcc.gov/media/radio/general-info-fm-tv-maps-data); [candidate query](../metadata.yaml); [reproducible inventory SQL](../research_notes/Radio%20reach%20data%20choices/method.md))

Start with verified in-force, in-scope FM/LPFM primaries. Check facility status, service, authorization type, effective dates, supersession and known silent status; “granted” can mean a construction permit. Record include/exclude/unresolved reasons and evidence, including any temporary operating authority. Exclude unbuilt proposals, alternatives and backup auxiliaries by default. Add translators/boosters only under an explicit policy with evidence of concurrent operation and, for network claims, rebroadcast linkage. Callsigns, the station index's filing numbers and an ownership-report respondent/FRN do not establish a dated ultimate owner. Crosswalk facilities to licensee, controlling owner and program network separately; keep disputed and missing links visible. **No eligible contour found means unknown, not zero.** ([FCC LMS](https://enterpriseefiling.fcc.gov/dataentry/public/tv/lmsDatabase.html); [FM Query](https://www.fcc.gov/media/radio/fm-query); [project station index](../station_index.csv); [ownership importer](../scripts/create_db.py); [station evidence template](../stations/WBHM.yaml))

Union concurrently eligible polygons for a facility, then union included facilities for each dated owner or network **before** computing weights. Two stations covering the same half of a BG cover half, not the whole: adding or capping station weights does not deduplicate residents. If a station alternative cannot be resolved, compute alternative scenarios rather than their union. Two owners may each count the same residents in their own reach; an all-owner total requires another union. For an overlap comparison, intersect the two owner unions and measure that footprint with the same allocation method. Label a sum of facility reaches as gross station-resident opportunities only if that metric is wanted. None of these counts measures unique listeners, market share, time spent, devices, actual programming heard, people present at broadcast time or grantee impact.

For an additive source count `Xg`, footprint reach is `Σ wg × Xg`. Fully contained units have weight one; disjoint units zero. A boundary touch is not positive covered area. Retain the unsimplified polygons, verify source datum and axis order, holes, multipart features and self-intersections; quarantine or document repairs and their area change. Split dateline crossings before planar indexing. Filter by bounding boxes, recheck exact geometry, then measure in a suitable regional equal-area CRS (e.g. EPSG:5070 for CONUS, 3338 for Alaska), not degrees squared or Web Mercator. Separate WGS84 display outlines from measurement geometry. ([TIGER geography definitions](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2024/TGRSHP2024_TechDoc.pdf); [GeoJSON coordinate and dateline conventions](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7946); [analysis details](../research_notes/Radio%20reach%20data%20choices/method.md))

| Partial-unit rule | Interpretation | Decision use |
|---|---|---|
| Internal point | Count all residents if the footprint covers the published representative point, otherwise none. Specify how boundary points are treated. The point need not mark a settlement; an ordinary centroid may even be outside. | Cheap baseline at block, BG or tract. Whole-BG jumps are especially misleading for small stations. |
| Land fraction | Intersected land area / total land area using the **same land mask**. Water-inclusive polygons divided by scalar `ALAND` do not implement this. | Transparent standalone allocation but assumes uniform density; coastlines, parks, campuses and rural housing clusters break it. Label a full-polygon fallback. |
| Block-supported ACS fraction | Within each reconciled ACS area, divide covered 2020 block support by total support. Weight blocks by residents for people, occupied units for households, all units for housing stock; use block points or fractional split-block land. | Best candidate for populated edges. Assumes the subgroup follows its proxy within the ACS area; prisons, new subdivisions and zero-support areas challenge it. Flag nonzero ACS counts over zero support; use a labeled land fallback or mark incomplete. |

For nonnegative counts, show the sum in wholly contained units and the sum in all positive-area-intersecting units as **conditional allocation bounds**. The gap tells an editor how many reported residents are in play at the edge; method spread is a sensitivity diagnostic, not a confidence interval. Even a block can be large. For DHC there is no survey sampling margin: enumeration coverage, nonresponse, imputation and differential-privacy disclosure avoidance still affect counts, especially small block cells. Aggregate small DHC/P.L. cells rather than presenting noisy block cross-tabs as exact household facts. ([DHC guidance](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2023/dec/2020-census-dhc.html); [coverage measurement](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/about/coverage-measurement.html))

**Keep ACS sampling error separate.** For fixed weights and distinct source estimates, approximate `MOE90 = sqrt(Σ (wg × MOEg)²)` assuming negligible covariance. Combine contributions from the same source cell *before* squaring. Prefer matched variance replicates when the table **and geography** are covered: apply identical weights to each of 80 replicates, recompute the statistic, then `SE = sqrt((4/80) × Σ(θr − θ)²)` and `MOE90 = 1.645 × SE`. Replicates cover 134 selected tables, not everything. Otherwise follow Census's derived-count, subset-proportion or general-ratio rules and label approximations. Ratios require separately allocated matching numerator and eligible denominator; zero denominator means undefined. Never average percentages, margins or medians. For income aggregate bin counts and report a median bracket, not an average of BG medians. Preserve release annotations and sentinels as missing, suppressed or undefined rather than zero; flag weak estimates and incomplete coverage. The ACS margin omits contour, allocation, time mismatch and nonsampling bias; interviews disrupted in 2020 also affect this period. ([ACS accuracy](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/accuracy/MultiyearACSAccuracyofData2024.pdf); [derived estimates handbook](https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/publications/2020/acs/acs_general_handbook_2020_ch08.pdf); [variance replicate documentation](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/replicate_estimates/2024/documentation/5-year/2020-2024_Variance_Replicate_Table_Documentation.pdf))

## Use a geometry engine for the build and plain files for Astro

**An index finds candidates; an engine clips and dissolves; a format stores results; a plugin displays them.** The existing FCC SQLite stores polygons as GeoJSON **text**, with an ordinary application/site index and no spatial tables. The probed macOS arm64 `data/.venv` used Python 3.15.2, SQLite 3.54.1 and Datasette 0.65.5. In-memory R\*Tree and Geopoly worked; extension loading could be toggled, but fresh connections lacked SpatiaLite and TG functions. Shapely, pyproj, GDAL Python, GeoPandas, DuckDB and sqlite-tg were absent from that environment; corresponding GIS CLIs were not found on the probed PATH. This is evidence about that interpreter, not every SQLite installation or a benchmark. Recheck locally with the command below. ([Local probes, query plans and SQL](../research_notes/Radio%20reach%20data%20choices/sqlite.md); [project dependencies](../pyproject.toml))

```sh
data/.venv/bin/python -c 'import sqlite3; c=sqlite3.connect(":memory:"); print(sqlite3.sqlite_version, c.execute("pragma module_list").fetchall())'
```

The [Astro site](../../website/src/pages/index.astro) is a hello-world page. Build a separate versioned `radio_reach.sqlite` sidecar offline, reading the FCC database read-only. Store the input manifest, eligible members and ownership evidence, footprint hash, source cells with universe/MOE, weights keyed by target/GEOID/method/support/vintage, and aggregate results with completeness flags. Index both target-first and GEOID-first lookups; validate before replacing a prior sidecar. A changed contour, owner grouping, geography or support requires new weights; new variables with the same universe can reuse them. Export a compact same-origin station/owner search index and per-target summary JSON and simplified GeoJSON; Astro can build accessible result pages and load an outline only on selection. Put the source period, contour snapshot, method, margin and partial-coverage flag beside the number. Do not transfer national blocks or the FCC database, call a visitor's localhost Datasette, or put Census API keys in client code. Introduce zoom-dependent tiles only if national browsing actually requires them.

## Conclusion

The next useful decision is **whether finer allocation changes what the site would tell a reader**. Freeze eligibility and ownership first; then pilot WBHM, a multiple-authorization facility, an overlapping primary/translator pair, a small urban station, and rural, coastal and interstate edges. Include non-CONUS or unsupported territory if in scope. Compare point, land and block-support estimates holding the footprint and release fixed; compare tract versus BG inputs, and test whole-block areas with known same-vintage totals. Check unique keys and unmatched rows both ways, mass conservation, weights in `[0,1]`, owner union between largest member and sum of members for additive nonnegative counts, holes, edge-only touches and repeatability. Record boundary population, fallback/missing share, sampling margin, method spread, runtime, memory and storage. Agree review triggers before seeing results—for example **5% in residents or 2 percentage points in a share**, as editorial thresholds, not accuracy guarantees. ([Pilot design and acceptance checks](../research_notes/Radio%20reach%20data%20choices/method.md); [engine verification cases](../research_notes/Radio%20reach%20data%20choices/sqlite.md))

If supported weights materially change populated edges, spend the extra build cost there; if simpler land weights agree across representative cases, publish the simpler method with its limitations. If authorization, ownership or geography remains unresolved, publish the mapped evidence and an explicit unknown instead of a definitive owner ranking. A smaller set of defensible, reproducible footprints is more useful than a national table whose apparent precision comes from counting the same residents twice.
